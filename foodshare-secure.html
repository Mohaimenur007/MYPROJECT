How it works

Three steps. Real impact.

From your kitchen to someone's plate — FoodShare makes food redistribution effortless and transparent.

01
List your surplus food
Fill a simple form with food details, quantity, pickup location and expiry. Takes under 2 minutes.
02
Charities get notified
Nearby charity organizations are instantly alerted. They browse, request, and schedule a pickup from the platform.
03
Accept, track & celebrate
Accept or decline requests with one click. Track every donation's journey and see your real-world impact grow.
Security first

Built with protection
at every layer

🔒
Login protection
Account lockout after 5 failed attempts. 15-minute cooldown. Prevents brute force attacks.
🔑
Strong passwords
Real-time password strength meter. Enforces uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and special characters.
🛡️
2FA for admins
Admin accounts require a Time-based One-Time Password for every login session.
⏱️
Session timeout
Automatically logs out after 30 minutes of inactivity. Keeps accounts safe on shared devices.
📋
Activity audit log
Every login, action, and security event is logged with timestamp and recorded for admin review.
🚨
Threat detection
Detects suspicious patterns like rapid requests, blocked accounts, and unauthorized admin access.
